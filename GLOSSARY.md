# Glossary

**Passenger.** A person who needs a seat on a licensed van.

**Driver.** A person who drives one usual road and can accept a passenger's ride.

**Ride.** One journey the passenger asks for. It is either one day or every working day of a month.

**Share the van.** The passenger rides with other people. The toll is split.

**Just me.** The passenger books the van alone. The fare is higher and the toll is not split.

**Opening price.** The first amount shown when the ride is sent. Either person can change it.

**Agreed fare.** The amount both the passenger and the driver accept.

**Confirmed ride.** The ride after both people agree the fare. It has a pickup number and a vehicle.

**Pickup number.** The digits the passenger shows the driver at the stop.

**Today's van.** The driver's list of people for the confirmed ride that day.

**On the van.** The driver has typed the passenger's pickup number.
